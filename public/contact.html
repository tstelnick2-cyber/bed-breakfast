Getting Here

Directions to Villa Maris

By Car from San Francisco

Take the Golden Gate Bridge north on US-101. Exit at Tiburon Blvd/E. Blithedale Ave. Continue east on Tiburon Blvd for approximately 5 miles into the town of Tiburon. Approximately 25–35 minutes from downtown San Francisco, depending on bridge traffic.

By Ferry from San Francisco

The most scenic option — take the Golden Gate Ferry from the San Francisco Ferry Building to Tiburon. A 20-minute crossing with views of the bridge, Alcatraz, and the Marin headlands. Villa Maris offers complimentary shuttle pickup from the Tiburon Ferry terminal.

From San Francisco Airport (SFO)

Take US-101 North from SFO. Cross the Golden Gate Bridge and exit at Tiburon Blvd. Approximately 45–60 minutes from SFO, depending on traffic. From OAK/SJC, cross the Bay Bridge to US-101 North — allow 60–75 minutes. Private car service available on request.
