Signature Experiences

Adventures on Water & Land

Every experience at Villa Maris is curated to put you in deeper relationship with this extraordinary corner of California. Our concierge arranges everything — you simply say yes.

On the Water

Private Bay Sailboat Charter

There is nothing quite like arriving at the Golden Gate by sail. Our certified captain takes guests on private sailboat excursions across Richardson Bay — morning voyages around Angel Island, afternoon sails toward the bridge, or golden-hour drifts as the city lights up across the water. Beverages and light bites provided aboard.

2–4 hours Up to 6 guests Year-round From $395/charter

Wellness

Sunrise Yoga on the Bay Deck

Each morning, as the bay awakens below and the first light catches Mount Tamalpais, our resident yoga instructor leads an hour-long flow session on our dedicated view deck. Vinyasa, restorative, and gentle practices available. All levels welcome. Mats, blocks, and straps provided. Meditation sessions available separately at dawn.

60–75 minutes Daily at sunrise Up to 8 guests Complimentary

Nature

Ring Mountain & Mt. Tamalpais Hikes

Marin County's trail system is world-class, and Villa Maris sits at its threshold. Our guide Simone leads guests on morning hikes up Ring Mountain — a 2,600-acre preserve with rare serpentine wildflowers and sweeping bay panoramas — as well as half-day treks up Mt. Tamalpais with its storied views of seven counties. Trail lunch provided.

2.5–5 hours Moderate difficulty 1–6 guests From $85/person

On the Water

Sea Kayaking & Paddleboarding

Launch from Tiburon's waterfront and paddle out into the glassy waters of Richardson Bay. Explore the hidden coves of Belvedere, drift past the Corinthian Yacht Club, or paddle all the way to Angel Island for a mid-morning shore break. Equipment and guides provided. Beginners and experienced paddlers equally welcome.

1.5–3 hours Calm bay waters Up to 4 guests From $75/person

Day Trips

San Francisco by Ferry

One of the great hidden pleasures of a Tiburon stay: hopping the Golden Gate Ferry for a 20-minute crossing to the Ferry Building, then spending the day in one of the world's great cities — before returning by water as the sun sets behind the Golden Gate. Our concierge arranges curated SF itineraries including art, food, and neighborhood walks.

20-min ferry Full day option Curated itineraries Shuttle to ferry

Nature & Wildlife

Angel Island Excursion

California's Ellis Island sits just a ferry ride away — a 740-acre jewel in the bay with panoramic views from its peak of all nine Bay Area counties. Hike its historic trails, discover the immigrant detention history, or simply picnic on a hillside as container ships drift past. Villa Maris arranges private guided tours and packed estate lunches for the journey.

Half or full day Ferry access Guided option Estate lunch included
