Our Story

A Hilltop Haven Above the Bay

Perched on Tiburon's most coveted hillside, Villa Maris is a private luxury guest estate unlike any other. From your window, San Francisco gleams across the water — the Golden Gate Bridge glows at dusk, and sailboats drift below as morning light washes over the Marin headlands.

The estate's eight individually designed suites blend coastal elegance with modern California living — vaulted beam ceilings, handcrafted stone soaking tubs, and private balconies that make every moment feel cinematic.

The Villa Maris Experience

Everything You Need. Nothing You Don't.

Bay Sailing

Private sailboat excursions on Richardson Bay with a certified captain. Sunset sails, day trips to Angel Island.

Sunrise Yoga

Morning sessions on the view deck led by our resident instructor. Flow with the bay as your backdrop.

Wine & Charcuterie

Curated Marin and Napa selections served fireside each evening. Local cheeses, charcuterie, and honey.

Guided Hikes

Ring Mountain and Mt. Tamalpais trails minutes from the estate. Guided half-day treks available upon request.
