Signature Amenities

The Highlights

From a bay-view hot tub under the stars to sunrise yoga on the deck, Villa Maris offers amenities that transform a stay into an experience.

Bay-View Hot Tub
Soak under the stars with the lights of San Francisco shimmering across the water.
Panoramic Fire Pit Deck
A wraparound deck with lava-stone fire pit and luxury outdoor seating — 180° bay views at every turn.
Multi-Level View Decks
Multiple terraced outdoor spaces, each framing a different sweep of the bay, bridges, and headlands.
